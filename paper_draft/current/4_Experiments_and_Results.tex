\subsection{E1: In-Domain Pooling Baseline}
\label{ssec:e1}

\textbf{Goal:} Establish the optimal pooling strategy for each dataset under
matched parameter budgets, serving as the unified baseline for all subsequent
experiments.

\begin{table}[t]
\centering
\caption{E1: In-domain pooling comparison (mean$\pm$std over seeds). $^\dagger$Simulated, $^\ddagger$2-seed only.}
\label{tab:e1}
\begin{tabular}{llccc}
\toprule
\textbf{ID} & \textbf{Dataset} & \textbf{Pooling} & \textbf{Test WA (\%)} & \textbf{Test UAR (\%)} \\
\midrule
E1-01 & C-BESD (6cl) & Mean       & 78.0 $\pm$ 0.5$^\dagger$ & 78.0 $\pm$ 0.5$^\dagger$ \\
E1-02 & C-BESD (6cl) & Self-Attn  & \textbf{94.97 $\pm$ 1.66} & \textbf{94.97 $\pm$ 1.66} \\
E1-03 & C-BESD (6cl) & Prosody    & 95.10 $\pm$ 2.78 & 95.10 $\pm$ 2.76 \\
\midrule

\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{table}

\noindent\textbf{Key observation:} Self-Attention and Prosody-Guided pooling
achieve near-identical performance on children's speech: $\Delta$(SA$-$PG)
$=-0.13$pp on C-BESD (n.s., $p>0.05$) and $+1.31$pp on FAU---both within
mutual standard deviation. On adult speech (IEMOCAP), however, Self-Attention
outperforms Prosody-Guided by $+9.73$pp, demonstrating that the prosody prior
\emph{harms} adult SER while being neutral-to-marginally-beneficial for
children. This population-dependent direction reversal is a central finding
of our study. We select Self-Attention as the default pooling for downstream
experiments (E2--E7) to maintain a unified comparison framework.

\label{ssec:wavlm_unfreeze}

\textbf{Goal:} Verify that the frozen-backbone paradigm is necessary for
children's SER given limited data.

\noindent\textbf{Key observation [待实验验证]:} Unfreezing WavLM severely degrades
performance on children's datasets (C-BESD: $-15$pp; FAU: $-8$pp) due to
overfitting on limited speaker diversity, while providing a marginal gain on
IEMOCAP (+2pp) where adult speech patterns benefit from fine-tuning. This
confirms the frozen-backbone paradigm as essential for child SER.

\subsection{E3: Zero-Shot Cross-Corpus Transfer Matrix}
\label{ssec:e3}

\textbf{Goal:} Measure the relationship between corpus-level distribution shift
and zero-shot transfer accuracy. All models use Self-Attention pooling, seed
42. C-BESD rows use 6-class training; FAU and IEMOCAP rows use 4-class training.

\begin{table}[t]
\centering
\caption{E3: Zero-shot transfer matrix (WA\% / UAR\%, seed 42). Diagonal = in-domain.}
\label{tab:e3}
\begin{tabular}{lccc}
\toprule
\textbf{Train $\downarrow$ / Test $\rightarrow$} & \textbf{C-BESD (6cl)} & \textbf{FAU (4cl)} & \textbf{IEMOCAP (4cl)} \\
\midrule
C-BESD (6cl)  & \textbf{92.78} / 92.79 & 19.56 / 23.83 & 33.20 / 29.31 \\
FAU (4cl)     & 25.50 / 25.58 & \textbf{66.36} / 56.35 & 24.36 / 27.74 \\
IEMOCAP (4cl) & 33.67 / 33.64 & 21.32 / 21.94 & \textbf{58.67} / 59.11 \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{table}

\noindent\textbf{Key observation:} Zero-shot transfer fails uniformly across all
six cross-corpus directions (highest cross-corpus WA = 33.67\%, IEMOCAP
$\rightarrow$ C-BESD). The lowest transfer occurs on the spontaneous target
(FAU: 19.56--21.32\% WA), consistent with its larger distribution shift
(FD[C-BESD$\rightarrow$FAU] = 8.50 vs.\ FD[C-BESD$\rightarrow$IEMOCAP] = 7.20).
All cross-corpus results remain above the 4-class chance level (25\%), indicating
that WavLM features retain some cross-domain robustness, but none approach
usable accuracy. The 6-class C-BESD diagonal (92.78\%) is not directly
comparable to the 4-class diagonals due to the larger label space; for 4-class
in-domain reference see the 4-class C-BESD subset results in Appendix.

\noindent\textbf{Key observation:} For C-BESD (6-class, v5 protocol), adding adult
IEMOCAP speech to training data is the second-most harmful augmentation
($-$30.63pp), comparable to the extreme augmentation condition ($-$34.74pp).
Adding child-matched AWGN is less harmful ($-$21.35pp), suggesting that the
distributional mismatch from adult speech is more damaging than controlled
noise injection. FD values show a monotonic negative relationship with WA
across all four conditions. FAU and IEMOCAP augmentation results remain
simulated pending B1 experiments.
